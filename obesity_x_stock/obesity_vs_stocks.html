Obesity prevalence vs. restaurant & fast-food stocks

Each chart indexes two series to 100 at the first year both are available, on a shared log-scaled axis so their relative growth stays comparable: a composite of publicly traded restaurant/fast-food stocks (equal-weighted, chained annual returns) against age-standardised adult obesity prevalence (BMI ≥ 30), averaged across sexes. Country selection is limited to the six countries with obesity trend data on file.

Methodology: for each country, the yearly composite is the equal-weighted average of each constituent ticker's year-over-year total-return (split/dividend adjusted Close from Yahoo Finance), chained into an index starting at 100. Early years for some countries are backed by very few constituents (see "tickers" count per year in the data table) since many of these companies only IPO'd recently — treat early-year moves with proportionally more caution. Obesity prevalence is the simple mean of the male and female age-standardised BMI≥30 rates published by NCD-RisC. The "Global" view composites all 65 fetched tickers across all six countries against the World obesity series. Ticker → country mapping and inclusion criteria: see obesity_x_stock/run_all_stock_exports.py and obesity_x_stock/build_country_summaries.py.
